# Cards

## Purpose and anatomy

Use a card to group related content or a focused task—not as a wrapper around every element. Anatomy may include header, content, metadata, actions, and footer.

## Rules

- Use `bg.surface`, `border.subtle`, `radius.lg`, and `elevation.subtle` only when separation helps scanning.
- Prefer one clear heading and one primary action.
- Keep card padding at 16–24px; reduce to 16px on small screens.
- If the entire card is interactive, make the interaction model obvious and provide a meaningful accessible name.
- Do not nest cards merely to create visual interest.
